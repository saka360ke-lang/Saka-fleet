1. Who we are

Saka Fleet ("we", "us", "our") is a WhatsApp-first fleet management service operated by Saka 360, a business registered in the Republic of Kenya. We are the Data Controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at any time at support@sakafleet.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what personal data we collect when you use our website (sakafleet.com), our API and app (app.sakafleet.com), and the Saka assistant on WhatsApp; why we collect it; how we protect it; and the rights you hold under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (KDPA) and — where it applies to you — the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. The data we collect

2.1 Data you give us directly

  • Account data: first name, last name, email address, and your WhatsApp phone number in international (E.164) format, including your country code. Your country code lets us serve you with the correct units, currency and date conventions.
  • Verification data: the 6-digit codes we email you, your consent record, and the fact that your email has been verified.
  • Fleet data: vehicle details you register — plate or serial number, make and model, year of manufacture, fuel type, VIN or chassis number (where applicable), odometer or hour-meter readings, and optional specifications such as insurance expiry dates, inspection dates and custom attributes.
  • Operational records: expense logs (type, amount, fuel volume, fill type, odometer, notes), earnings records, service reminders, driver assignments, compliance documents (licences, PSV/CPC/operator cards, insurance policies) and any photographs of receipts or documents you send.
  • Communications: the messages you exchange with the Saka WhatsApp assistant, including support requests.

2.2 Data generated automatically

  • Chat metadata: timestamps, message direction (sent/received), and session state needed to resume your conversation.
  • Telemetry: anonymised or pseudonymised technical logs — response latency, error events, and AI usage records (purpose, tier, token counts — never the content of private conversations beyond what is needed to operate a turn).
  • Payment references: when you pay for a subscription, our payment processor shares transaction references and status with us. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Website telemetry: see section 11.

2.3 Data we do not collect

We do not collect biometric data, precise location data (beyond what is voluntarily in your messages), or sensitive category data under the KDPA, except where you choose to send compliance documents as part of your fleet operations.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and operate your account and let Saka serve you on WhatsApp.
  • To verify your identity and your email address when you sign up or perform sensitive actions (assigning drivers, transferring passbooks, deleting data).
  • To store your fleet records, compute fuel economy and costs, schedule reminders and generate the reports you request.
  • To keep the service healthy: monitoring uptime, diagnosing faults, preventing abuse and improving reliability.
  • To bill you through our payment processor and honour subscriptions and trials.
  • To reply to your support questions.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Legal bases

We process your personal data only where a lawful basis exists:

  • Performance of a contract: operating your account, storing your fleet records and delivering reports — the service you asked for.
  • Consent: your explicit agreement to our Terms and this Policy when you sign up, and your consent record for verification emails. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, preventing fraud and abuse, and diagnosing technical faults — always balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation: retaining records where Kenyan law or applicable regulations require it.

5. Who we share data with

  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database and storage), Google Cloud (Cloud Run hosting and logging), and Resend (transactional email). They process data only on our instructions.
  • Communication platforms: Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API, which delivers the messages between you and Saka, subject to Meta's own policies.
  • Payment processors: Paystack, which processes subscription payments and shares transaction status with us.
  • AI model providers: our assistant may route some turns to language-model providers to understand and answer you; calls are purpose-limited and logged in our usage telemetry.
  • Authorities: where disclosure is required by law or a valid legal order.

We never sell or rent your personal data. Our sub-processors are contractually bound to process data only for the purposes we specify.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and fleet records: for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period after deletion for legal and audit purposes.
  • Chat transcripts: retained to keep conversations coherent; you may request deletion of your account and its transcripts at any time.
  • Verification codes: 10 minutes, after which they expire and cannot be reused.
  • Technical telemetry: aggregated or deleted on a rolling basis as operational needs dictate.
  • Historical logs: deleting a vehicle preserves its historical logs (a feature of the service), which are removed only with account deletion.

7. How we protect your data

  • Encryption: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest in our database and storage.
  • Access controls: strict row-level security in the database means one user can never read another user's records; secret credentials are never exposed to browsers or the website.
  • Verification-first design: email OTP verification gates sensitive actions such as driver assignment and passbook transfers.
  • Monitoring: automated health checks, rate limiting and anomaly detection run continuously around the service.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, but we apply strong, industry-standard safeguards and keep them under continuous review.

8. International transfers

Saka Fleet serves a global audience. Our infrastructure providers operate in regions including Europe, the United States and Africa. Where personal data leaves Kenya, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the providers' contractual commitments and recognised adequacy frameworks. Your country of residence may have additional protections; contact us for the specifics that apply to you.

9. Your rights

Under the KDPA and (where applicable) the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data (you can also edit most records directly in WhatsApp).
  • Erase your data ("delete my account" in WhatsApp, or email us).
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Port your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any right, email support@sakafleet.com from the email on your account, or message Saka on WhatsApp. We respond within 30 days. In Kenya, you may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

10. Children

The service is intended for individuals of legal age to contract (18 in most jurisdictions; 16 in some). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Website telemetry

Our marketing website does not use advertising cookies, trackers or analytics pixels. The only network calls the site makes are: (a) the signup and verification calls to app.sakafleet.com you initiate, and (b) the live status check against app.sakafleet.com/api/health, which transmits no personal data. Fonts load from Google Fonts under Google's privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced on the website and, where appropriate, through the WhatsApp assistant.
