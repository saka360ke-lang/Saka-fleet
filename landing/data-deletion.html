Your right to deletion

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 and — where it applies to you — the GDPR, you may ask us to delete your personal data. Here is exactly how that works with Saka Fleet.

What you can delete, and how

1. Your whole account — the fastest way

Message Saka on WhatsApp and type delete my account. Saka verifies your identity with a 6-digit email code, then removes:

  • Your profile (name, email, WhatsApp number, preferences);
  • Your chat history and conversation transcripts;
  • Your vehicles, financial ledgers, receipts and compliance records;
  • Reminders, driver links and passbook records tied to you.

Note: a vehicle you have sold retains its permanent VIN/passbook record with its new owner — vehicle history is an ownership record, not personal data about you.

2. One specific record

You can already correct or delete individual ledger entries yourself: message Saka my entries, then edit or delete the entry number (a full before/after record is kept for your protection for 30 days).

3. By email

Email support@sakafleet.com from the address on your account with the subject "Data deletion request" and your WhatsApp number. We respond within 30 days.

What happens after deletion

  • Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — please export any Apex reports you want to keep first.
  • We may retain the minimum legally required records (for example, payment records under tax law) for as long as the law requires, then delete them.
  • Aggregated, anonymised telemetry (which can never identify you) may remain for system health.
