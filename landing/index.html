Questions, answered

Anything else? Email support@reports.sakafleet.com.

What is Saka Fleet?

Saka Fleet is your car's friend on WhatsApp. Chat in plain language to register your car, log fuel and expenses, update mileage, set reminders and get tidy PDF reports — no separate app required. Trucks, bikes, boats, generators and EVs are welcome too.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account starts a 30-day welcome trial with everything unlocked — up to 5 vehicles, unlimited reports, drivers and documents. No card is required. The trial begins when you register your first car with Saka on WhatsApp.

Do I need to install an app?

No. WhatsApp is the whole interface. The website is for signup, pricing and company information; daily operations happen entirely in chat.

What can I log on WhatsApp?

25 expense types (fuel, charging, maintenance, parts, tyres, repairs, parking, tolls, fines, insurance, licences, security, loading, weighbridge, hire and more), earnings, odometer or engine-hour readings, service reminders, driver assignments, compliance documents and receipt photos.

Which vehicles or assets does Saka Fleet support?

Cars, motorcycles, trucks, buses, heavy equipment (excavators, loaders), machinery, generators, boats and electric vehicles. Road assets use odometer and km/L; EVs use kWh and km/kWh; machines, generators and boats use engine hours and L/h — every metric matches the asset.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data lives in an encrypted cloud database with strict access rules, and processing complies with the data protection laws that apply to you, including the GDPR where applicable. Your records are never sold or shared.

Where are my receipt photos stored?

Saka reads your receipt, logbook or licence photo and saves the numbers. The original file is stored on paid plans and during the welcome trial; on the Free plan only the parsed figures are kept. Delete any receipt photo from WhatsApp with receipts, then delete receipt N.
