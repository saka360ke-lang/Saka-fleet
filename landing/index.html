Questions, answered

Anything else? Email support@sakafleet.com.

What is Saka Fleet?

Saka Fleet is a WhatsApp-first vehicle assistant. You chat with Saka in plain language to register your vehicle, log fuel and expenses, update the odometer, set service reminders and receive branded PDF reports — no separate app required.

How does the free trial work?

Every new account starts a 30-day welcome trial with full Solo Pro features. No card is required. The trial begins when you register your first vehicle with Saka on WhatsApp.

Do I need to install an app?

No. WhatsApp is the whole interface. The website is for signup, pricing and company information; daily operations happen entirely in chat.

What can I log on WhatsApp?

19 expense types (fuel, charging, maintenance, parts, tyres, repairs, parking, tolls, fines, insurance and more), earnings, odometer or engine-hour readings, service reminders, driver assignments, compliance documents and receipt photos.

Which vehicles or assets does Saka Fleet support?

Cars, motorcycles, trucks, buses, heavy equipment, machinery, generators and boats. Road assets use odometer and km/L; machines and generators use engine hours and L/h — every metric matches the asset.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Data lives in an encrypted cloud database with strict access rules, and processing complies with the data protection laws that apply to you, including the GDPR where applicable. Your records are never sold or shared.
